Privacy Policy & File Access Terms

Transparency regarding on-device data processing, file storage, and advertising.

Last Updated: February 2025 • Version 1.0
📄

1. Local File Processing & Offline Security

KaraOkeGO Song Compiler operates 100% locally on your device. Your karaoke database files (KaraGOblob, KaraGOidx) and imported audio/MIDI files (.mid, .kar, .mp3) are processed, compiled, and edited entirely offline within your device's memory.

We do not collect, transmit, or upload any of your song databases, file metadata, or personal media to external servers. Your files remain under your complete control at all times.

📁

2. File Access & Storage Acknowledgement

To fulfill its primary function as a karaoke database compiler, KaraOkeGO requires local file system access permissions on your device.

✅
By using KaraOkeGO Song Compiler, you acknowledge and agree that the application requires local storage permissions to output compiled files (KaraGOblob & KaraGOidx) directly to your device's file system (e.g., under Files -> On My iPhone -> KaraOkeGO Compiler or Android local app folder).

File access permissions are strictly restricted to reading user-selected song input files and writing compiled output databases. No other user directories or personal documents are accessed.

📺

3. Advertising & App Tracking Transparency

To keep KaraOkeGO Song Compiler free for all users and support continuous feature development, this application integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Google AdMob may utilize standard mobile advertising identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or Advertising ID on Android) where permitted by user consent settings to deliver relevant banner and interstitial ads.

You can manage or modify your ad tracking preferences at any time through your device system settings:
• iOS: Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Tracking
• Android: Settings -> Security & Privacy -> Privacy -> Ads
