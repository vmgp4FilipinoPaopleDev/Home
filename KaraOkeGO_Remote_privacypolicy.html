KaraOkeGO Remote
Privacy Policy
🛡️ 1. Overview
KaraOkeGO Remote is designed to serve as a local Wi‑Fi remote controller for KaraOkeGO and SongHub karaoke host applications. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information while you use it.
📶 2. Local Network Usage
The application requires Local Network access solely to discover and communicate with your KaraOkeGO or SongHub player on your local Wi‑Fi network using Port 48080.

No karaoke control data, commands, searches, or interactions are transmitted outside your local network, except for standard advertising-related requests made by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).
👤 3. No Personal Data Collection
KaraOkeGO Remote does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), including:
  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Physical Address
  • Location Data
  • Contact Information
📢 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)
KaraOkeGO Remote uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner and interstitial advertisements.

Google may collect anonymous device identifiers and limited usage information as permitted by Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework and Google's own privacy practices.

We do not have access to or control over data collected directly by Google AdMob.
📧 5. Contact & Support
If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the KaraOkeGO development team through the support channels provided with the application.
🔄 6. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements, legal requirements, or service changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
