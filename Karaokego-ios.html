Privacy Policy for KaraOkeGO

Effective Date: October 14, 2026
Last Updated: October 14, 2026

KaraOkeGO ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our iOS application KaraOkeGO (the "App") handles your information.

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Data

KaraOkeGO does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address). No account creation or sign-in is required to use the App.

B. Local Data Storage

All app preferences, custom song databases (KarGOidx), custom imported karaoke files (KarGOblob), favorite songs, song queue reservations, custom background media, and audio settings are stored locally on your device (using local storage and iOS Documents directory). This data never leaves your device.

2. On-Device Device Permissions

The App requests the following optional iOS device permissions solely for local, real-time app features:

  • Microphone Access: Used locally on-device for real-time audio playback, pitch detection, and performance scoring. Your voice audio is analyzed live on-device and is never recorded, stored remotely, or transmitted to external servers.
  • Camera Access: Used locally on-device to display a live camera preview background option while singing. Camera feeds are rendered strictly on your screen and are never recorded, saved, or transmitted.
  • Photo Library & File Access: Used strictly to allow you to select custom background photos/videos or import custom karaoke index/payload files from your device.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Third-party advertising partners may collect anonymized device identifiers (such as IDFA, subject to your permission) and non-personal diagnostic data to serve and measure advertisements.

For more information on how Google AdMob collects and uses data, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Children’s Privacy

KaraOkeGO does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The App is designed for general audiences and family entertainment.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

📒 App Instructions

KaraOkeGO is .mid, .kar and compress karaoke file Player

1. Compress Files (*blob & *idx)

Add your favorite song in Settings. You can upload compress/compiled karaoke file (such as KaraGOblob file), you must also import the matching *idx song index file (e.g. KaraGOidx file). The index file contains the catalog listing and song numbers required for searching and playing songs.

2. Standalone MIDI & KAR Files (.mid & .kar)

You can import individual .mid or .kar files directly from your device. KaraOkeGO automatically extracts the synchronized lyrics and adds the song to your catalog.

Note: Importing individual .mid and .kar files uses uncompressed phone memory.

3. KaraOkeGO Song Compiler App (Save Space)

To save phone storage space, you can use another app (KaraOkeGO Song Compiler App) to compile your entire .mid and .kar song collection into compressed KaraGOblob and create an index list KaraGOidx files before importing them into KaraOkeGO.
